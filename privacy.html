Zane Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Zane is a personal assistant application. Its developer built it for his own use, and he is its only user. It isn't offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What Zane can access

Zane can access only what the account owner explicitly allows through Google's consent screen. Zane asks for a permission only when the feature that needs it is switched on.

  • Google account email address: to confirm which account is connected.
  • Gmail, read-only: to search and summarise messages when the owner asks.
  • Gmail drafts: to create draft replies for the owner to review. Zane never sends email.
  • Google Calendar: to read events on calendars the owner owns, and to create events the owner has approved. Zane doesn't change or delete existing events, and doesn't invite anyone.
  • Google Drive, read-only: limited to the folders the owner chooses.

How data is used

  • Only to carry out a request the owner makes, at the time he makes it.
  • To answer a request, the content it needs may be sent to Anthropic's API, which generates the answer. It isn't sent to any other AI provider.
  • Zane doesn't use Google user data to develop, improve or train AI models.
  • Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Storage and security

  • Zane doesn't store the content of emails, events or documents. It keeps only its own replies and a log of technical metadata, such as counts and decisions.
  • Google sign-in tokens are encrypted and stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Connections to Google use HTTPS, and only to a fixed list of read and draft endpoints.

Removing access

"Disconnect" in Zane deletes the stored tokens and revokes Zane's access at Google. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Zane's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
